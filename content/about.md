I'm a security engineer based in Seoul, working where security operations meets LLM systems.

**Currently** — Security and AI engineering at a SIEM/SOAR vendor. My work there is the security layer of an observability platform and its LLM assistant: auth on model-query endpoints, SSRF guards on generated SQL, PII and secret masking in prompt logs, and a golden-set regression gate that a release has to pass before it ships.

**Previously** — Backend and security engineering at a data-security vendor, on a personal-data detection product. Cut large-file API response time by 80% with a Redis cache layer, and extended detection to unstructured data with an NER model.

**Research** — First author on a digital forensics paper at CISC-W'25, awarded the KISTI Director's Award.

**Languages** — Korean (native), Japanese (JLPT N1), English (working).

## What this site is for

Write-ups where I ran the thing and measured it, rather than summarizing what other people measured.

The bias is toward what a result costs — the layer that had to be tuned, the metric that hid the damage, the number that looked good and meant nothing. Those parts get dropped from most write-ups, and they are usually the parts that matter when you have to make a decision.

Posts are in English; some have Korean versions.
